Retrieval Association Evaluation

A retrieval_association is valid only if:

- appears in recruiter searches
- appears in job descriptions
- appears in ATS searches
- improves retrieval coverage

Removing the retrieval_association
reduces retrieval coverage
but does not change anchor meaning.

## Evaluation Metrics

For every anchor:

Measure:

Coverage Score
Density Score
Frequency Score
Freeze Spot Count
Dead Phrase Count

Coverage Score

Job descriptions matched
÷
total job descriptions

Density Score

Total hits
÷
matched job descriptions

Freeze Spot Count

Phrases never matched.

Dead Phrase Count

Phrases matched below threshold.

Acceptance

Coverage >= 80%

Freeze Spots <= 10%

Dead Phrases <= 20%

Average Density >= target
