Metadata: relations.generation.rule.txt

## Purpose

Mandatory population requirements.

Every anchor defined in
profile.source.types.ts
must generate:

adjacent
recruiter_surfaces.en
recruiter_surfaces.vi
retrieval_associations

Empty arrays are invalid.

Generation requirements are owned by:
relations.generation.rule.txt

Coverage requirements are owned by:
relations.coverage.rule.txt

Validation requirements are owned by:
relations.validation.rule.txt

## ReplacementGenerationRule

When replacing retrieval_associations,
the generator shall preserve approximate lexical density.

Replacing low-quality associations with higher-quality
associations is preferred over deleting entries.

Large reductions in retrieval_association cardinality
require corpus-supported justification.
